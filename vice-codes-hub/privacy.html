Vice Codes Hub Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Vice Codes Hub is owned and operated by Dade-Made Studios LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the Vice Codes Hub iPhone app (the “App”), including its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the choices you have. In this policy, “Dade-Made Studios LLC,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Dade-Made Studios LLC, which is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here.

Vice Codes Hub is an unofficial, fan-made app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

The short version: the App has no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your favorites, checklist progress, and settings are saved only on your own device. The App downloads a public content file from our website, and it shows small banner ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and ad-interaction data, and uses your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not have user accounts, sign-up, sign-in, chat, or social features. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, or payment card details. The App has no in-app purchases. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Data Saved on Your Device

Your favorites, launch checklist progress, platform choice, and settings are stored locally on your device. They are shared only with the App’s own Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets through an on-device App Group, so the widgets can show your information. This data is not sent to us, and we do not run servers that store user profiles.

Because this data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. We cannot recover it for you.

3. Content Updates from Our Website

To keep codes, news, and countdown information current, the App downloads a public content file from vicecodeshub.com, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. No personal information is sent with this request. As with any web request, the hosting provider may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, the time of the request, and basic device or browser information. This is governed by GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The App does not use browser cookies or Google Analytics. If you visit vicecodeshub.com in a web browser, the website’s cookies, analytics (Google Analytics 4, off until you accept, with Global Privacy Control honored), and ads (Google AdSense, which may be personalized subject to Google’s consent message in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland and the Do Not Sell or Share opt-out in the U.S.) are described in the Website Privacy Policy, and you can change your choice with Cookie settings in the footer of any page.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to use and shows small banner ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC) through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

When the App requests or shows an ad, Google may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • device identifiers and device and app information
  • your IP address
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)
  • the advertising identifier for your device (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see Section 5)

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow tracking, show ads that are more relevant to you. Google’s use of this information is governed by its own policies. See how Google uses information in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any information that identifies you personally from Google; we may receive aggregated reports (such as total ad views and revenue).

5. App Tracking Transparency (Your Choice)

The App asks for your permission using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before your device’s advertising identifier can be used for tracking.

  • If you tap “Allow”: Google may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and measure ad performance across apps.
  • If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”: your advertising identifier is not shared. You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized or contextual. The App works exactly the same either way.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.

6. How to Control Your Data

  • Turn off tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn off Vice Codes Hub (or “Allow Apps to Request to Track”).
  • Delete your saved data: uninstall the App. This removes your favorites, checklist progress, and settings from your device.
  • Questions about data held by Google: use the links in Section 4, or email us and we will help point you in the right direction.

7. Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not keep user data on our own systems. Data saved by the App stays on your device until you delete the App. Information collected by Google AdMob, and any technical logs kept by our website host, are kept according to their own privacy policies. If you email us, we keep your email and message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then delete it.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Your U.S. State Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in California or other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have rights to know about, access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information or of targeted advertising. We do not sell your personal information. Since we do not hold personal information from the App, most requests are best handled through the controls in Section 6 or directly with Google. You can also send a request by email to mdecoded303@gmail.com, and we will respond within the time required by law. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

10. Security

The App communicates with our website and with Google over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

The App contains links to YouTube, Linktree, and the App Store. These open in your browser or the relevant app, and those services’ own privacy policies apply. Ads shown in the App may also link to other apps or websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google AdMob and YouTube)
  • Linktree Privacy Notice
  • Apple Privacy Policy (App Store)
  • GitHub Privacy Statement (website hosting)

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when the law requires it. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we may also let you know in the App or on the App Store page. Continuing to use the App after an update means the updated policy applies.

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Vice Codes Hub can be sent to Dade-Made Studios LLC at mdecoded303@gmail.com.
