Vice Codes Hub Support

What Is Vice Codes Hub?

Vice Codes Hub is a free, unofficial, fan-made iPhone companion app by Dade-Made Studios LLC. It collects cheat codes, tips, news, a launch countdown, and a launch checklist, with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. It has no accounts or sign-in, and your favorites and progress stay on your device.

Vice Codes Hub is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Frequently Asked Questions

A code isn’t working. What should I do?

Make sure you picked the right platform (PlayStation, Xbox, or PC) in the App, and enter the code exactly as shown, during gameplay and not in a menu. Some codes only work in certain game versions or editions, and game updates can change or remove them. Codes may also disable achievements or trophies, so save first. If a code still looks wrong, email us with the code name, your platform, and your game version, and we will check it.

How do I add the widget?

Install and open the App once. Then touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap the + (Edit > Add Widget) button, search for Vice Codes Hub, pick a size, and tap Add Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the widget area, and add Vice Codes Hub. If a widget looks empty, open the App once so it can refresh.

Why are there ads, and how do I control tracking?

The App is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. When iOS asks whether to allow tracking, you can choose either option; the App works the same, and if you decline you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What data does the App collect?

We do not collect your name, email, location, or other personal information. Your favorites, checklist progress, and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I request removal of content?

If you own content or a trademark and want something removed from the App or this website, email mdecoded303@gmail.com with the subject line “Takedown request”, tell us where it appears, and include the details listed in our Terms of Use. We review every request promptly.
