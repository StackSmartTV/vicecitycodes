Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Our Commitment

Dade-Made Studios, LLC wants vicecodeshub.com and our apps, Vice Codes Hub and Super Squad Dash, to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. Accessibility is ongoing work, and weAccessibility is ongoing work, and we keep improving it with each update.rsquo;re working to improve it over time.

Our Approach

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 from the W3C as a reference when we design and build the Site and our apps. This statement is not a claim that every page or screen fully meets those guidelines.

What We Do Today

On this website:

  • A “Skip to content” link and labeled navigation for keyboard and screen reader users.
  • A visible focus outline on links and buttons, and a menu that can be closed with the Escape key.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Reduced animation when your device’s “reduce motion” setting is turned on.

In the Vice Codes Hub app (iPhone):

  • VoiceOver labels on buttons and other controls.
  • Most text scales with your iPhone’s text size setting (Dynamic Type). We’re working to extend this to the remaining screens.
  • Decorative animations are reduced when Reduce Motion is turned on.

In Super Squad Dash: the game is still in development. We’re working to add accessibility options, and we will update this statement as they become available.

Known Limitations

Some parts of the Site and our apps may not yet be fully accessible. Some content comes from third parties and is outside our control, including embedded YouTube videos and players, and ads served by Google. Their accessibility depends on those providers.

Report a Barrier

If something on the Site or in one of our apps is hard to use, please email us at mdecoded303@gmail.com. It helps to tell us the page or app screen, what you were trying to do, and the device, browser, or assistive technology you were using. We read all accessibility feedback and use it to improve future updates.
