GTA 6 is out now: launch-day fixes, a record 99 on Metacritic, and the free Leonida map

Topics:
  • Launch & sales
  • Updates & fixes
  • Cheats & glitches

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. What Rockstar and Take-Two confirmed today, how to fix PSN and download problems, the record reviews, the free map and tonight's bonus deadline.

In this article (6 sections · 3 min)
  1. Confirmed: the highest-rated game on Metacritic
  2. Resolved: two short PSN outages
  3. Confirmed: the free Leonida map and poster are live
  4. Last call: the Vintage Vice City Pack ends tonight
  5. Yes, there are cheats (with a catch)
  6. The soundtrack, awards and what comes next
  7. Sources

It is finally here. Grand Theft Auto VI is out now on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two made it official this morning with a press release titled "Rockstar Games Announces Grand Theft Auto VI Now Available." The game unlocked at midnight local time country by country, starting in New Zealand, so players on the US East Coast got in at 12:00 AM ET. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams started an hour later, at 1 AM ET. There is still no PC version announced. Here is what is confirmed today, what is only reported, and how to fix the most common launch problems.

Confirmed: the highest-rated game on Metacritic

The review embargo lifted on Wednesday, November 18, and the scores are huge. Polygon reports GTA 6 debuted at a 99 Metascore from more than 150 critic reviews, tying the original Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest score on the site, and a 98 on OpenCritic. Take-Two's launch release calls it "the highest rated game in history" and quotes IGN: "Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable." Not every review is perfect, though: Australia's ScreenHub gave it an 80 and found some early sections boring. Metacritic says user reviews open at 12 PM Pacific (3 PM ET) on November 20.

Resolved: two short PSN outages

PlayStation players had a bumpy run-up. On Wednesday afternoon, Sony's status page warned that players "might have difficulty launching games, apps or network features," and Axios reports it showed everything fixed by 2:30 PM ET. TechRepublic, citing the outage monitor IsDown, adds a second disruption of about 24 minutes starting around 12:34 AM ET on launch night. Both are resolved, and Sony has not said what caused them. Axios says Xbox players reported no problems on Wednesday.

If the game still will not start for you, the problem is probably on your console, not Sony's servers. Our new fix finder below has the official steps from PlayStation Support and Xbox Support. Pick your console and tap your problem.

Can't play GTA 6? Fix finder

Launch-day problems and the official fixes. Pick your console, then tap a problem.

  • PS5Crashes to the home screen with error CE-108255-1
    1. No confirmed fix yet (Nov 25): the Nov 23 patch notes list general crash fixes but do not name this error. Install the update, then save manually and often.
    2. Check for a game update (Options > Check for Update) and update the PS5 system software.
    3. Players report it sometimes helps to Restore Licenses, clear cache in Safe Mode, or set Video Output from Manual to 1080p.
    4. Details: our first-weekend report.

    Source: GameSpot: CE-108255-1 crash reports

  • PS5"Services are busy" or you cannot sign in to PSN
    1. Check the official PlayStation Network status page first. If it shows an alert, the problem is on Sony's side, so wait and try again.
    2. PSN had two short outages around launch (Nov 18 and Nov 19). Both were resolved.

    Source: PlayStation Network status

  • PS5The download is stuck or failed
    1. Open Downloads/Uploads in the control center, select GTA VI, then Pause and Resume.
    2. Still stuck? Select Cancel and Delete, find the game in your Game Library and download it again.
    3. Last resort: start the PS5 in Safe Mode and choose Clear Cache and Rebuild Database.

    Source: PlayStation Support: GTA VI troubleshooting

  • PS5It asks for a system update
    1. Go to Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings and install the latest version. Sony released one just before launch.

    Source: PlayStation Support: GTA VI troubleshooting

  • PS5Padlock icon or a license error
    1. Make sure you are signed in to the account that bought or redeemed the game.
    2. Go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses.
    3. Playing on another user's copy? The owner must turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play on this PS5.

    Source: PlayStation Support: GTA VI troubleshooting

  • PS5 + XboxNot enough storage space
    1. Rockstar says the game needs up to 212 GB, plus room for updates.
    2. Use our storage checker to see what to delete.

    Source: Rockstar Newswire: pre-load and file size

  • XboxXbox will not connect or the game will not start
    1. Check the official Xbox status page. If a service is down, wait for it to recover.
    2. Restart the console fully (hold the power button for 10 seconds), then try again.

    Source: Xbox status

  • Xbox"You need to own this game" on Xbox
    1. Sign in with the account that bought the game.
    2. If you play on a different profile, set this console as the buyer's Home Xbox: Settings > General > Personalization > My home Xbox.

    Source: Xbox Support: Designate your home Xbox

  • XboxYou only have an Xbox One
    1. GTA 6 does not install on Xbox One. You can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming (started 1 AM ET on Nov 19), at Xbox Series S settings.
    2. You still need to buy the game, plus Game Pass or cloud playtime.

    Source: Xbox Wire: GTA VI on Xbox Cloud Gaming

  • PS5 + XboxCheat codes do nothing
    1. GTA 6 cheats only work after you finish the story. Using one turns off saving for that session and disables trophies and achievements.
    2. Full list and button inputs: GTA 6 cheat codes.

    Source: Vice City Codes: GTA 6 cheat codes (sources listed on the page)

One warning: Yahoo reports that a few players who switched their console region to New Zealand to play early say their accounts were banned. Those are unverified user claims, but changing regions is not worth the risk, especially now that the game is out everywhere.

Confirmed: the free Leonida map and poster are live

Boxed copies only contain a download code, so there is no paper map in the box. Instead, Rockstar promised in its November 3 Newswire post that a free cover-art poster and Leonida map would go up on its site on launch day, and they are now in the Media section of the official GTA VI site in screen and printable versions. Rockstar also plans a double-sided printed poster on the Rockstar Store shortly after launch, for $0.99 on its own or free with any Rockstar Store purchase.

Last call: the Vintage Vice City Pack ends tonight

If you have not bought the game yet, today matters. PlayStation Support says the Vintage Vice City Pack comes with all digital purchases made before 11:59:59 PM local time on November 19. It includes a '55 Vapid Stanier with a Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach, retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt. US prices have not changed: $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, per Take-Two.

Yes, there are cheats (with a catch)

Our GTA 6 cheat codes page lists the 17 codes that match in at least two of PC Gamer, GameSpot and PowerPyx, with PS5 and Xbox inputs. The catch: cheats do nothing until you finish the story, and using one turns off saving for that session and disables trophies and achievements. So play it straight first.

The soundtrack, awards and what comes next

  • The Album: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album from Atlantic Records, with 34 original tracks, hit streaming services at midnight, with vinyl and CD at gtavi-thealbum.com (Take-Two).
  • The Game Awards: GTA 6 leads with eight nominations, including Game of the Year. The show is on Thursday, December 10, at 7:30 PM ET (GameSpot).
  • The Festival: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival is this Saturday, November 21, at Mana Wynwood in Miami (Rockstar Newswire).
  • The score: Justice's original score arrives on December 11.
