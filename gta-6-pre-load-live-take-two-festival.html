GTA 6 pre-load is live, Take-Two calls pre-orders "unprecedented," and Rockstar books a Miami festival

You can download GTA 6 right now, Take-Two's last earnings call before launch was all about record pre-orders, Rockstar detailed PS5 Pro and cloud support, and a two-stage launch festival hits Wynwood on Nov 21.

Nine days before Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks on November 19, 2026, the waiting has turned into downloading. Pre-load opened overnight on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, Take-Two used its final pre-launch earnings call to talk up record demand, and Rockstar announced a launch-weekend music festival in Miami. Here is what is confirmed, with sources at the bottom.

Pre-load is open now

Rockstar's Newswire post Pre-Load Grand Theft Auto VI Now went up on November 10. Everyone with a digital pre-order can start downloading today. If you bought the code-in-box version, you can redeem the code and start pre-loading as soon as you have it; those boxes reach retailers from November 12.

The timing matched what Rockstar promised on November 3. PS5 opened at midnight local time, and Xbox opened worldwide at 12 a.m. GMT, which was 7 PM Eastern on Monday, November 9. The download needs up to 212 GB depending on your console. Leave auto-downloads on and keep the console in Rest Mode (PS5) or Sleep Mode (Xbox) so it can finish overnight. Not sure you have the room? Use the checker below.

Will GTA 6 fit on your console?

Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load file needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console (Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3, 2026). Final install sizes can differ a little, so leave some extra room.

Where do I find my free space?
  • PS5: Settings → Storage → Console Storage.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Settings → System → Storage devices.

A record on PlayStation, and a rolling midnight launch

Sony's PlayStation Blog says GTA 6 is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store. That is why pre-load starts nine days early. Sony also confirmed a rolling midnight launch by country on PS5. It begins at 12:00 AM NZDT in New Zealand and moves around the globe as each country hits midnight on November 19. For most of the US, that means the game unlocks at your local midnight going into Thursday.

PS5 Pro, cloud play and Remote Play

Alongside a new pre-load trailer, Rockstar shared its first official technical notes, as reported by Gematsu and Game Informer:

  • 30 frames per second is the target on both consoles. Rockstar says it chose that to keep Leonida's density and detail intact.
  • PS5 Pro adds PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) for a sharper image, plus ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets.
  • On PS5, DualSense haptic feedback runs through the whole game and can be adjusted in the accessibility settings, and audio uses Tempest 3D Audio.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming will stream the game to Xbox Series X|S and even Xbox One consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings.
  • Remote Play is fully supported on PlayStation and Xbox.

There is still no PC version announced.

Take-Two: "unprecedented" pre-orders, finished game

Take-Two reported its fiscal second quarter (July to September) on November 9. Net bookings were $1.60 billion, down from $1.96 billion a year earlier and a little under its own guidance. That quarter ended before launch, so it tells us nothing about GTA 6 sales yet.

The forward-looking part was all GTA 6. CEO Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders are "continuing to reach unprecedented levels" and are "tracking far in excess of our initial expectations." Take-Two kept its full-year outlook of $8.0 to $8.2 billion in net bookings and guided $3.35 to $3.5 billion for October to December, the quarter that includes launch. GamesBeat reports executives said Rockstar has finished the game. Zelnick also refused to celebrate early, saying the company still is not "ready to declare victory."

You may also see a number like 7.71 million digital pre-orders. That is a Sensor Tower estimate (as of November 3, via Reuters), not an official Take-Two or Rockstar figure.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, Nov 21 in Wynwood

On November 9 Rockstar announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, presented with PlayStation, on Saturday, November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami. It is produced by the team behind III Points and runs on two stages. The lineup, which Rockstar describes as artists from the game and its official soundtrack:

  • Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and SoFaygo
  • Cardi B, Metro Boomin, Tainy, Suero, Rauw & Friends, and Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso
  • Justice (DJ set), Nick León B2B DJ Python, Prospa B2B Skream, and The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set)

Tickets go through a lottery. Entries closed today, November 10 at 4 PM ET, and winners are texted a code by 7 PM ET. Code holders can buy on DICE from Wednesday, November 11 at 12 PM ET, first come, first served. The DICE listing shows tickets at $60, a 4 PM door time, a limit of two per person, and no transfers. The event is 17+. If you missed the lottery, DICE runs a wait list for returned tickets. There is no on-site parking, so plan on rideshare.
