Park Crown Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

Park Crown is owned and operated by Dade-Made Studios LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use the Park Crown mobile app for iPhone (the “App”), a pickup basketball scorekeeper, and the choices you have. “Dade-Made Studios LLC,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Dade-Made Studios LLC, which is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here. The rules for using the App are in our Terms of Use.

The short version: the App has no accounts and no sign-ups. We do not run a server for the App. We do not use analytics SDKs, and the App has no ads. Camera video and audio are processed on your device to keep score, track possession, flag possible contact and possible violations, and make highlights. Nothing from the App is uploaded to any server we operate. Stats, game history, King of the Court leaderboards, and highlights stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the App. You share content only when you tap Share. Park Crown Pro subscriptions are billed by Apple through your Apple ID.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App has no user accounts, sign-up, or sign-in. We do not ask you to create a profile, and we do not run a server that stores player accounts or game data. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or ads in the App. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or payment card details in the App. We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share App data with advertising or analytics companies.

2. Camera, Microphone, and On-Device Processing

The App uses the camera, including video and audio, to keep score, track possession, flag possible contact and possible violations, and make highlights. This processing happens on your device. Camera video and audio are not uploaded to any server we operate. We do not run a server for the App.

Flags and foul votes are suggestions for the players to decide. The App never makes referee calls. Our Terms of Use explain this in more detail, along with your responsibility to get consent from people you film.

3. Voice Commands and Speech Recognition

The App uses the microphone and speech recognition for voice commands. Where available, the App uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition, so spoken words are processed on your device. If on-device speech recognition is not available, Apple may process your speech under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your speech on any server we operate, and we do not run a speech-recognition service.

4. Player Identification

The App identifies players during a session only by appearance matching, such as shirt color and similar visual cues. The App does not use face recognition. Appearance data is kept only for the current session and is discarded when the session ends.

5. Data Saved on Your Device

Stats, game history, King of the Court leaderboards, and highlights are stored only on your device, using Apple’s SwiftData storage. They are not sent to us. Because this data lives on your device, deleting the App deletes it. We cannot recover it for you.

6. Sharing, Location, and Calendar

You share content only when you tap Share. That can include the system share sheet, text invites, and .ics calendar files. When you share, the apps or services you choose (for example, Messages) handle that content under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not upload shared content to a server we operate.

Location and calendar are used only when you tap to use a feature that needs them, if at all. You can refuse those permissions. The App does not use location or calendar to track you in the background.

7. Subscriptions

The App offers an optional Park Crown Pro subscription, billed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We never see or store your card number or billing details. Apple tells the App whether your subscription is active so the App can unlock Pro features. Apple’s handling of your payment information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. Prices, the free trial, auto-renewal, cancellation, and refunds are described in our Terms of Use.

8. Data Retention

We do not keep App data on our own systems. Camera and speech processing happen on your device as described above, or, for speech, by Apple when on-device recognition is not available. Appearance-matching data is discarded when the session ends. Stats, game history, King of the Court leaderboards, and highlights stay on your device until you delete the App. If you email us, we keep your email and message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then delete it.

9. Children’s Privacy

Park Crown is rated 13+ and is intended for users 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Your Privacy Rights (California, Other U.S. States, EEA, and UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access, correct, or delete it, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information as those terms are defined by laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell personal information for money, and we do not share App data with advertising or analytics companies for targeted advertising.

Because we do not hold App data on our own systems, most requests are handled on your device: you can delete stats, history, leaderboards, and highlights by deleting the App, and you can deny or change camera, microphone, speech, location, or calendar permissions in iOS Settings. You can also email mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for using your privacy rights. If you live in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

The App processes camera, microphone, and game data on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this data. When you buy or manage a subscription, or when Apple processes speech because on-device recognition is not available, that communication is with Apple. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum and do not collect App data onto our own systems.

12. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple services for the App Store and subscriptions, on-device or Apple speech recognition, and the system share sheet. If you share content, the app or service you share to has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of Apple or of apps and services you choose to share with.
