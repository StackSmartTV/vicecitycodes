Gloopernova

Coming to the App Store for iPhone.

Gloopernova is a cosmic arcade game. Steer Gloop, a hungry space blob, through bright galaxies, swallow anything smaller than you, dodge anything bigger, and grow until you can gulp down planets. Play eight galaxies, Black Hole bonus rounds, and an Endless mode with a Game Center leaderboard.

Free to play with ads and optional in-app purchases. Gloopernova is rated 13+ and is intended for users 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed to children.
