Gloopernova Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Gloopernova is owned and operated by Dade-Made Studios LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the Gloopernova mobile game for iPhone (the “Game”), and the choices you have. “Dade-Made Studios LLC,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Dade-Made Studios LLC, which is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here. The rules for using the Game are in our Terms of Use.

The short version: the Game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. We do not run a first-party analytics server. Purchases are handled by Apple. Leaderboards use Apple Game Center if you are signed in. The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, with Google AdMob as an ad network. Before any ad loads, Google’s consent message is shown where the law requires it, and then iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt. The ad SDKs may collect your device advertising identifier only if you allow tracking, plus other device and ad data described below.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game has no user accounts, sign-up, sign-in, chat, or social features. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. We do not sell your personal information for money.

2. Game Data Saved on Your Device

Your progress and settings (for example Goo coins, shields, unlocked galaxies, skins, upgrades, daily reward streak, local high scores, and sound and Reduce Flashing settings) are stored locally on your device using standard app storage. They are not sent to us, and we do not run servers that store player profiles.

Because this data lives on your device, deleting the Game deletes it. We cannot recover it for you. Items bought from the App Store that are not used up (such as Remove Ads) can be restored with Restore Purchases.

3. Apple Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the Game can submit your Endless mode score to a Game Center leaderboard and show the leaderboard. Game Center is operated by Apple. Apple handles your Game Center player identity, nickname, and scores under your Apple Account and the Apple Privacy Policy. The Game does not ask you to sign in; if you are not signed in, the Game keeps scores on your device only. We do not copy your Game Center data to any server we operate.

4. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases: shield packs (used up as you play), Remove Ads, and the World Flags skin pack. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your card number or billing details. Apple tells the Game which items you bought so it can deliver them, and lets the Game restore non-consumable items with the Restore Purchases button. Apple’s handling of your payment information is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, Google AdMob, and Unity Ads)

The Game is free to play and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Unity Technologies’ ad mediation service. LevelPlay requests ads from ad networks, which are ironSource Ads and Google AdMob (Google LLC), and may include Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Rewarded ads (for example, a revive or an extra shield) are always optional. A full-screen ad may show after a run ends; buying Remove Ads turns those off. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and are not child-directed.

Before any ad is requested:

  • If you are in a region where consent is required (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland), the Game shows Google’s consent message (User Messaging Platform, or UMP), and your choice is passed to the ad networks.
  • iOS then shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

To show, cap, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the ad SDKs may collect:

  • your device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the ATT prompt (see Section 6)
  • the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and other device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, and network type
  • IP address and the approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped
  • crash, performance, and other diagnostic data from the ad SDKs

Those third parties use this information for advertising and measurement under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), including ironSource Ads: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform: Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services)
  • Unity Ads (when it serves through LevelPlay): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

We do not receive this advertising information on a server we operate. Ad partners may give us aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown), which do not identify you.

6. Consent, Tracking, and Your Choices

  • If you allow tracking: the ad SDKs may use your IDFA to show more relevant ads and measure them across apps and websites owned by other companies.
  • If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”: your IDFA is not shared. You still see ads, but they are less personalized. The Game works the same.
  • Change tracking any time: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.
  • Change your ad consent: in the Game, open Settings > Privacy Choices. This button appears where Google’s consent rules require it.
  • Limit personalized ads from Apple: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the Game.
  • Questions about data held by an ad partner: use the links in Section 5, or email us and we will help point you in the right direction.

7. Data Retention

We do not keep player data on our own systems. Game data stays on your device until you delete the Game. Game Center data is kept by Apple. Information collected by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Google AdMob, and Unity Ads is kept according to their own privacy policies, linked in Section 5. If you email us, we keep your email and message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then delete it.

8. Children’s Privacy

Gloopernova is rated 13+ and is intended for users 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed to children under 13, and ad requests are marked as not child-directed. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Your Privacy Rights (California, Other U.S. States, EEA, and UK)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access, correct, or delete it, to object to or restrict certain uses, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information as those terms are defined by laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell personal information for money. Allowing ad partners to use your advertising identifier for personalized ads may count as “sharing” under some state laws; you can opt out at any time by turning off tracking (Section 6) or using Privacy Choices in the Game.

For people in the EEA and UK, the legal basis for personalized advertising is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through Privacy Choices; the ad partners may rely on legitimate interests for limited purposes such as fraud prevention and ad delivery. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold personal information from the Game, most requests are best handled with the device controls in Section 6 or directly with the ad partners in Section 5. You can also email mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for using your privacy rights.

10. Security

The Game communicates with Apple and our ad partners over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we use established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

11. Third-Party Links and Services

Ads in the Game may link to other apps or websites, and the Game’s Credits screen links to music license pages. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), including ironSource Ads: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform: Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services)
  • Unity Ads (when it serves through LevelPlay): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes or when the law requires it. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we may also let you know in the Game or on the App Store page.
