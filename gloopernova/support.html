Gloopernova Support

Need help with Gloopernova? Email Dade-Made Studios LLC at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can, usually within 2 business days.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Game, go to the Store, and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy, and that you are connected to the internet. Remove Ads and the World Flags skin pack come back. Shield packs are used up as you play, so they cannot be restored.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the Game deletes your progress.

How do leaderboards work?

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, your Endless mode scores go to the Gloopernova leaderboard. Tap GAME CENTER on the High Scores screen to see it. If you are not signed in, your scores are still kept on your device.

Why do I see ads? Can I choose not to be tracked?

Ads keep the Game free. Rewarded ads are always optional, and Remove Ads turns off the ads that show after a run. When iOS asks whether the Game can track you, you can choose “Ask App Not to Track” and the Game works the same. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Flashing and motion

Nothing in the Game flashes more than 3 times a second. Turn on Reduce Flashing in Settings to soften glows and turn off screen shake.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email mdecoded303@gmail.com with your device model and iOS version, the Game version (bottom of the Settings screen), what happened, and a screenshot or screen recording if you can.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
