GTA 6 Map: Leonida and Vice City Locations Confirmed So Far

Every GTA 6 region and location Rockstar has officially named: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga, plus smaller spots.

How to read the labels: Confirmed = stated by Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft. Reported = reported by a reputable outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar. Not confirmed = not announced or unknown.

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, and its biggest city is a modern-day Vice City, the series' version of Miami Confirmed. Rockstar calls it "Vice City, USA. The darkest side of the sunniest place in America." This guide gathers every region and location Rockstar has officially named so far, mostly from its "Only in Leonida" pages and the Ultimate Edition descriptions. We are writing this from Miami, so where a real-world comparison helps we will say so, but we have labelled those comparisons as our own reading, not Rockstar's.

One important caveat before we start: the full map has not been released. Rockstar's November 3 Newswire post said a printable map of Leonida will be posted on the official site as a free download on November 19. Until then, nobody outside Rockstar knows exactly how the regions connect or how big the world is. You will see map-size claims online. Rockstar has not published an official size figure, so we are not repeating any number.

The six regions Rockstar has named

Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" site presents six destinations across the state Confirmed. Here is each one with Rockstar's own description.

Vice City. "We're a long way from the '80s, but Vice City is still the sun and fun capital of America." Rockstar describes "the glamour, hustle, and greed of America captured in a single city," and names specific neighborhoods and landmarks: the pastel art deco hotels and white sand of Ocean Beach, the panaderías of Little Cuba, the bootleg brands of the Tisha-Wocka flea market, and the VC Port, "the cruise ship capital of the world." Our reading: Ocean Beach is the game's version of Miami Beach and South Beach, and Little Cuba echoes Little Havana. Rockstar did not say that directly.

Leonida Keys. "The dress code is casual, the bars are loaded." Rockstar calls the Keys a tropical archipelago where life "isn't flashy but it's easy," right "on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America." This is where Jason's story begins: his official bio says he ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners.

Grassrivers. "The untamable jewel of Leonida's crown." Rockstar describes a primordial wetland where "the gators may be the most famous attraction, but there are far deadlier predators and weirder discoveries among the mangroves." It is clearly the game's answer to the Everglades.

Port Gellhorn. "Leonida's forgotten coast." Rockstar paints a faded resort town of cheap motels, shut-down attractions and empty strip malls, with "a new economy" fueled by "malt liquor, painkillers, and truck stop energy drinks." Rockstar suggests jumping on a dirt bike there, and holding onto your wallet.

Ambrosia. "The battle for the health and wealth of Leonida begins here." In the heart of the state, Rockstar says, "the Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs, while the local biker gang provides almost everything else." It reads as the rural, industrial interior of the map.

Mount Kalaga. "Room to breathe on the state's northern fringes." Rockstar calls it a national landmark near the northern border with prime hunting, fishing and off-road trails, where "hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals live far from the prying eyes of the government." The Ultimate Edition's '67 Vapid Dominator buggy is pitched specifically for "the backwoods of Mount Kalaga and beyond."

Smaller places named in official material

Rockstar's edition and pre-order descriptions mention a handful of specific spots Confirmed. They give a sense of the map's texture:

  • Shore Drive and Shore Court: the Vintage Vice City Pack's '55 Vapid Stanier comes with a personal garage at Shore Court, "a stone's throw" from Ocean Beach. The '95 Grotti Cheetah is described as an "ode to Shore Drive."
  • Watson Bay: home of the Paradise Garage that comes with the Ultimate Edition buggy, with a weapon locker and a place to stash stolen goods to be fenced.
  • Lake Leonida: location of One-Eyed Willie's, an off-road mod shop.
  • Stockyard: home of the Electric Fang tattoo parlor and the Stock 305 clothing store.
  • Gambit Bay and Washington Beach: named in the description of the Ultimate Edition's Squalo boat.
  • Southside Vice City: home turf of the PTT Youngin$ gang, whose compound you can raid in the Ultimate Edition.

Rockstar's Launch Gallery, published November 5, also includes scenes labelled in its image descriptions as a Fleeca bank robbery and a night at a bar called The Rusty Anchor.

What the map tells us about how you will travel

The places Rockstar chose say a lot about the variety of vehicles. Boats keep showing up: the Ultimate Edition includes a Squalo boat and a kayak, Brian Heder runs a boat yard in the Keys, and Cal Hampton listens in on Coast Guard radio. Off-road trails in Mount Kalaga and dirt bikes in Port Gellhorn suggest plenty of time away from paved roads. Polygon's coverage of Rockstar's details also mentions public transportation and changing weather, which we will check once we can play.

Is the map the "biggest ever"?

Rockstar has called GTA VI "the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet." That is a quote about the game as a whole, not a measured map size Confirmed. Fan estimates based on trailer footage circulate widely, but they are estimates, so we have left them out Not confirmed. We will update this section with real numbers once the official map is out and players have measured it.

Real Miami, fictional Leonida

Rockstar's marketing has leaned on the real city. Branded beach umbrellas, a Vice City logo on the Kaseya Center, and the Miami HEAT's "A Night in Vice City" game on November 18 all point to Miami. We cover those stunts in our Miami takeover report. Just remember that Leonida is a satire. Street names, businesses and neighborhoods are invented, even when they rhyme with real places.

Coming after launch

Once the free map is published and we have played, we will add a region-by-region breakdown with our own screenshots, travel tips and points of interest. Until then, meet the people who live here in our GTA 6 characters guide.
