GTA 6 pre-load moves up to Nov 10, and Rockstar confirms it needs up to 212 GB

Rockstar moved digital pre-loading two days earlier and gave the first official file size. Here are the exact times for PS5 and Xbox, what stays on Nov 12, and the rest of this week's confirmed GTA 6 news.

In this article (5 sections · 4 min)
  1. Exact pre-load times for PS5 and Xbox
  2. 212 GB is now official
  3. A free GTA 6 poster and map on launch day
  4. Also this week: weather and spoiler rules
  5. Still not confirmed
  6. Sources

Update, Nov 7: Rockstar has since opened The Launch Gallery with a collection of new screenshots, and Take-Two's CEO addressed delays and piracy at WSJ Tech. Monday's earnings call is the last scheduled investor update before launch. Read the latest.

Two weeks before Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026, Rockstar has answered the two questions we get asked most: when can I start downloading it, and how much space do I need? On November 3, a new Rockstar Newswire post moved digital pre-loading up to Tuesday, November 10, two days earlier than the November 12 date Rockstar gave when pre-orders opened in June. It also gave the first official size. The pre-load file needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console.

Exact pre-load times for PS5 and Xbox

The two consoles do not start at the same moment, so check which one applies to you:

  • PlayStation 5: digital pre-loading starts at midnight local time on November 10, in your own time zone. Players further east get it first, so New Zealand starts hours before the US. Sony's PlayStation Blog gives the same date.
  • Xbox Series X|S: pre-loading starts worldwide at the same moment, 12 a.m. GMT on November 10. In the US that is 7 PM Eastern (4 PM Pacific) on Monday, November 9, as VGC points out. So in the Americas, Xbox owners can start a few hours before PS5 owners.
  • Boxed copies: unchanged. The code-in-box versions for both consoles should be in stores from November 12. The box holds a download code, not a disc, so you redeem it and start the pre-load from there.

Pre-loading only gets the files onto your console early. The game itself still unlocks on November 19, and Rockstar has not changed that date. You need a digital pre-order to pre-load. If you have not ordered yet, purchases made before November 20 still include the Vintage Vice City Pack, and digital pre-orders add a free month of GTA+.

212 GB is now official

Last week we covered a reported 210 GB figure from a PlayStation storage tracker. Rockstar's own number is a little higher. The Newswire says the pre-load file "will require up to 212 GB depending on your device." Rockstar did not give separate PS5 and Xbox numbers, and GamesRadar+ notes that pre-load downloads and final install sizes can differ slightly. Sony's guide tells PS5 owners to clear a minimum of 212 GB.

That is a big chunk of a launch PS5's roughly 667 GB of usable space, and well over half of the roughly 364 GB an Xbox Series S has free out of the box. Do the clean-up this weekend, not at midnight on pre-load day. Our checker below now uses the official figure.

Will GTA 6 fit on your console?

Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load file needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console (Rockstar Newswire, Nov 3, 2026). Final install sizes can differ a little, so leave some extra room.

Where do I find my free space?
  • PS5: Settings → Storage → Console Storage.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Settings → System → Storage devices.

A free GTA 6 poster and map on launch day

Rockstar is also bringing back a series tradition in digital form. On November 19, a printable GTA 6 cover art poster and a map of Leonida will be added to the official site as a free download. Shortly after launch, a double-sided printed version will be sold at the Rockstar Store for $0.99, or included free with any Rockstar Store purchase. Rockstar has not said whether the code-in-box retail version includes a paper map.

Also this week: weather and spoiler rules

More details keep coming out of Edge's cover story. On November 4, GamesRadar+ highlighted what Rockstar's Aaron Garbut told Edge about the weather. He called it "much more granular" than the usual rain-or-sun system. Wind moves trees and waves, and islands and buildings block it, so an anchored boat rocks differently depending on where it sits. Storms can shake planes and helicopters and even damage them a little if you stay in one too long, and Leonida's residents run for shelter when bad weather rolls in.

If you plan to share clips after launch, note Rockstar's video guidelines on its support site. As Metro reported, they are not new (the page was last updated in January 2025), but they are doing the rounds again. Rockstar says it may take down videos of pre-release footage, the game's ending, and edited compilations of cutscenes or plot dialogue. Cutscenes shown as part of a longer playthrough are allowed.

Still not confirmed

  • Review date: November 18 is still only reported, by NateTheHate and Insider Gaming. Rockstar has not announced it.
  • GTA 6 cheat codes: none exist yet. We will post them here the moment real ones are verified after launch, not before.
