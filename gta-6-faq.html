Short answers come first, and the related guides go deeper. We will revise this page after the game launches on November 19, 2026, and the "last updated" date above will change when we do.

When does GTA 6 come out?

Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S Confirmed. On PS5, Sony says the launch rolls out at midnight country by country, starting in New Zealand. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams start at 1 AM Eastern.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

No PC version has been announced Not confirmed. Rockstar Support lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Reports of a later PC release rely on unnamed sources, not Rockstar or Take-Two.

Can I play GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

Not on PS4. PlayStation says it is PS5 only. On Xbox One you can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1 AM ET on November 19, using Series S graphics settings, but you still need to buy the game.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US, $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition Confirmed. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB depending on your console Confirmed. A roughly 1 GB pre-launch update (1.002.000) began rolling out on November 13 Reported. Leave some extra room for future patches.

Does the GTA 6 box come with a disc?

No. Rockstar Support says the physical version contains a download code, not a disc. Redeem the code to download the game.

Can you buy houses in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not confirmed whether you can buy houses or other property Not confirmed. What is confirmed: the pre-order bonus includes a personal garage at Shore Court near Ocean Beach, the Ultimate Edition adds the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay and "Jason's safehouse vehicles," and Jason's official bio says he lives rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties. We will update this answer after launch.

Is GTA 6 multiplayer? Is there crossplay?

Take-Two describes GTA VI as "a single-player experience" Confirmed. Rockstar has not detailed an online mode for GTA VI on its official pages. Polygon notes there is no cross-platform play between PlayStation and Xbox.

Who are the main characters?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple pulled into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida. Rockstar has also introduced Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, the Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. See our characters guide.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida, and its biggest city, a modern-day Vice City (based on Miami). Rockstar has named six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published an official map size Not confirmed. A free printable Leonida map is due on Rockstar's site on November 19, according to its November 3 Newswire post. We will update this answer once players can measure it.

Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps?

Rockstar's stated target is 30 fps on all consoles, including PS5 Pro Confirmed. PS5 Pro adds PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows.

Are there GTA 6 cheat codes?

None are confirmed yet Not confirmed. Any list you see before launch is fake. Our GTA 6 cheats page explains how GTA cheats have worked and will list real codes once they are verified.

When do GTA 6 reviews come out?

A November 18 review embargo has been reported by Insider Gaming Reported. Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Can you switch between Jason and Lucia?

Rockstar has not explained how switching between the two leads works Not confirmed. Both are playable protagonists, and Edge's preview describes a Criminal Profile that tracks them both. We will answer this properly after launch.

Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

Not confirmed Not confirmed. Rockstar's pre-order bonus includes a weapon pattern inspired by his palm-tree shirt, and the Ultimate Edition revolvers come "from the Vercetti Estate," but Rockstar has not said that Tommy himself appears.

What is in the Ultimate Edition?

Rockstar Support lists extra vehicles (including a '95 Grotti Cheetah and a '67 Vapid Dominator buggy), personalized weapons, outfits, extra shops and mod garages, a gang compound raid and a Classic Car Collection side job, unlocked across the story. Standard owners can buy the upgrade later.

Will GTA 6 come with a map?

Boxed copies are code-only and do not include a printed map, according to NME. Rockstar's November 3 Newswire post said a free printable Leonida map and cover poster will be posted on its site on November 19, with a printed version sold at the Rockstar Store afterward.

What is the pre-order bonus, and when does it end?

The Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage, retro outfits and hairstyles, and a weapon pattern. It comes with pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026. Digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA+.
