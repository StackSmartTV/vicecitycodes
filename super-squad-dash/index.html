Super Squad Dash

Coming soon to the App Store.

Super Squad Dash is a fast, comic-style superhero runner. Dash down the lanes with your squad, power up through gates, tag in a different hero mid-run, and take down a boss at the end of every level. Bonus minigames like Flick Shot and Hero Slash reward skilled play with extra coins.

Free to play with optional in-app purchases. For ages 12 and up (13+ on the App Store).
