Super Squad Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Super Squad Dash is owned and operated by Dade-Made Studios LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the Super Squad Dash mobile game (the “Game”), and the choices you have. In this policy, “Dade-Made Studios LLC,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Dade-Made Studios LLC, which is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here. The rules for using the Game are in our Terms of Use.

The short version: the Game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. We do not run a first-party analytics server. Purchases are handled by Apple. The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, using Google AdMob and Unity Ads. On first launch, after the photosensitivity notice, people in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland see Google’s consent form, and then iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, before any ad loads. Those ad SDKs may collect a device advertising identifier only if you allow it, plus coarse location, ad interaction, and crash and performance data, and they use that information for advertising and measurement.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not have user accounts, sign-up, sign-in, chat, or social features. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Game Progress Saved on Your Device

Your game progress (for example coins, unlocked heroes and skins, upgrades, missions, and settings) is stored locally on your device using standard app storage. It is not sent to us, and we do not run servers that store player profiles.

Because your progress lives on your device, deleting the Game deletes this saved data. We cannot recover it for you.

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (such as cosmetic hero skins). All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your card number or billing details. Apple tells the Game which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them with the Restore Purchases button. Apple’s handling of your payment information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, Google AdMob, and Unity Ads)

The Game is free to play and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Unity Technologies’ mediation service. LevelPlay requests ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) and Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Rewarded ads are optional. Ad content is capped at Teen.

On first launch, after the photosensitivity notice and before any ad loads:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform, or UMP).
  • iOS then shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt.

The ad SDKs may collect:

  • your device advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see Section 5)
  • coarse location (based on your IP address)
  • ad interaction data
  • crash and performance data

Those third parties use this information for advertising and measurement. Their privacy policies:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services)
  • Unity Ads: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

The Game has no accounts and no first-party analytics server. We do not receive this advertising information on a server we operate.

5. Consent and App Tracking Transparency

You can change your ad consent with Privacy Choices in the Game’s Settings. That control is shown where it is required. You can change tracking at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone or iPad.

  • If you allow tracking: the ad SDKs may use your device advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising and measurement.
  • If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”: your advertising identifier is not shared. You can still see ads. Rewarded ads stay optional.

6. No Accounts and No First-Party Analytics

The Game has no accounts and no first-party analytics server. It does not send gameplay or player data to a server we operate. Your progress stays on your device, as described in Section 2. Advertising and measurement in Section 4 are done by those third-party ad SDKs, not by us.

7. How to Opt Out and Control Your Data

  • Change ad consent: open the Game, go to Settings, then Privacy Choices. This control is shown where it is required.
  • Turn off tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn off Super Squad Dash (or “Allow Apps to Request to Track”).
  • Limit personalized ads from Apple: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the Game. This removes the progress saved on your device.
  • Questions or requests about data held by an ad partner: contact that partner using the links in Section 4, or email us and we will help point you in the right direction.

8. Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal information through the Game, we do not keep player data on our own systems. The Game has no accounts and no first-party analytics server. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. Information collected by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Google AdMob, and Unity Ads is kept according to their own privacy policies, linked in Section 4. If you email us, we keep your email and message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then delete it.

9. Children’s Privacy

Super Squad Dash is rated 13+ and is intended for users 13 and older. It is not designed for or directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in California, other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the use of your personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Since we do not hold personal information from the Game, most requests are best handled through the controls in Section 7 or directly with the ad partners in Section 4. You can also email us and we will respond within the time required by law.

11. Security

The Game communicates with Apple and our ad partners over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

Ads shown in the Game may link to other apps or websites. The Game relies on Apple for purchases, and on Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Google AdMob, and Unity Ads for advertising. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes or when the law requires it. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we may also let you know in the Game or on the App Store page. Continuing to play after an update means the updated policy applies.

14. Contact Us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Super Squad Dash can be sent to Dade-Made Studios LLC at mdecoded303@gmail.com.
