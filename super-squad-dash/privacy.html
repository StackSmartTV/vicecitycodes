Super Squad Dash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Super Squad Dash is owned and operated by Dade-Made Studios LLC, a Florida limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the Super Squad Dash mobile game (the “Game”), and the choices you have. In this policy, “Dade-Made Studios LLC,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Dade-Made Studios LLC, which is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here.

The short version: the Game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Your progress is saved on your own device. Purchases are handled by Apple. The Game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, including ads from Unity Ads and Google AdMob, and those ad partners may collect device and ad-interaction data; they use your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not have user accounts, sign-up, sign-in, chat, or social features. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment card details. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Game Progress Saved on Your Device

Your game progress (for example coins, unlocked heroes and skins, upgrades, missions, and settings) is stored locally on your device using standard app storage. It is not sent to us, and we do not run servers that store player profiles.

Because your progress lives on your device, deleting the Game deletes this saved data. We cannot recover it for you.

3. In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (such as cosmetic hero skins). All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your card number or billing details. Apple tells the Game which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them with the Restore Purchases button. Apple’s handling of your payment information is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and Google AdMob)

The Game is free to play and shows ads, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to double your coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels or minigame rounds. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), a service of Unity Technologies, and the ad networks that LevelPlay works with. The ad networks currently used are Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads are limited to content suitable for ages 12 and up.

When the Game requests or shows an ad, LevelPlay and its ad network partners may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, screen size)
  • your IP address and the approximate (city or region level) location derived from it
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, tapped, or completed)
  • diagnostic and performance data (such as crash or load-error information related to ads)
  • the advertising identifier for your device (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see Section 5)

These partners use this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, detect fraud and abuse, and, if you allow tracking, show ads that are more relevant to you. Their use of this information is governed by their own privacy policies, including the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy (which cover LevelPlay/ironSource and Unity Ads) and the Google Privacy Policy (which covers AdMob; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services). We do not receive your advertising identifier or any information that identifies you personally from these partners; we may receive aggregated reports (such as total ad views and revenue).

5. App Tracking Transparency (Your Choice)

On iOS 14.5 and later, the Game asks for your permission using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before your device’s advertising identifier can be used for tracking. The Game shows this prompt after you finish your first level, not when you first open the app, and ads start only after you answer.

  • If you tap “Allow”: ad partners may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads and measure ad performance across apps.
  • If you tap “Ask App Not to Track”: your advertising identifier is not shared. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. The Game works exactly the same either way.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone or iPad.

6. Gameplay Analytics

The Game does not currently send gameplay analytics to us or to any analytics service. If we add anonymous gameplay analytics in a future update (for example, to see which levels are too hard or how many coins are earned, using a service such as Unity Analytics), it will not include your name or contact details, and we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before it is turned on.

7. How to Opt Out and Control Your Data

  • Turn off tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn off Super Squad Dash (or “Allow Apps to Request to Track”).
  • Limit personalized ads from Apple: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the Game. This removes the progress saved on your device.
  • Questions or requests about data held by an ad partner: contact that partner using the links in Section 4, or email us and we will help point you in the right direction.

8. Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal information through the Game, we do not keep player data on our own systems. Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. Information collected by Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners (including Unity Ads and Google AdMob) is kept according to their own privacy policies. If you email us, we keep your email and message only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then delete it.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Game is rated for ages 12 and up (13+ under the App Store’s current age ratings) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has given us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact us at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in California, other U.S. states, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the use of your personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Since we do not hold personal information from the Game, most requests are best handled through the controls in Section 7 or directly with the ad partners in Section 4. You can also email us and we will respond within the time required by law.

11. Security

The Game communicates with Apple and our ad partners over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we choose established providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

Ads shown in the Game may link to other apps or websites, and the Game relies on third-party services described above (Apple, and Unity LevelPlay with its ad networks Unity Ads and Google AdMob). We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties. Please review their privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy (including LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads)
  • Google Privacy Policy (Google AdMob)

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes or when the law requires it. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new effective date, and for significant changes we may also let you know in the Game or on the App Store page. Continuing to play after an update means the updated policy applies.

14. Contact Us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Super Squad Dash can be sent to Dade-Made Studios LLC at mdecoded303@gmail.com.
