Super Squad Dash Support

Need help with Super Squad Dash? Email Dade-Made Studios LLC at mdecoded303@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can, usually within 2 business days.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Game and go to Settings, then tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy, and that you are connected to the internet. Your paid skins will unlock again.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Your progress is saved on your device. There are no accounts, so progress does not sync between devices, and deleting the Game deletes your progress. Paid skins are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any device with Restore Purchases.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email mdecoded303@gmail.com with:

  • your device model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • the Game version (shown at the bottom of the Settings screen)
  • what happened and what you were doing (level, hero, minigame)
  • a screenshot or screen recording if you can

Why do I see ads? Can I choose not to be tracked?

Ads keep the Game free. Rewarded ads are always optional. When iOS asks whether the Game can track you, you can choose “Ask App Not to Track” and the Game works the same. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
