GTA 6 after one week: an estimated 53 million sold, why you can't replay missions, and how the ending works

Topics:
  • Launch & sales
  • Updates & fixes

Analysts put GTA 6 at about 53 million copies in its first week. Rockstar explains why there is no mission replay and how the post-story game works, plus the Black Friday price check and the Rockstar union vote.

  2. Confirmed: why there is no mission replay
  3. Confirmed: how the ending works (light spoilers)

  5. Rockstar's UK staff vote against union recognition
  6. Still true: single-player only, no official PC version
  7. Sources

Updated November 28, 2026. GTA 6 has been out for nine days, and there was no new patch this week. The latest update is still the November 23 one with seven fixes. Instead, the news this week was about how big the launch is and how the game is built. A new analyst estimate puts first-week sales above 50 million copies, and Rockstar developers explained two design choices players keep asking about: why you cannot replay missions, and what happens when the story ends. Here is what is official, what is an estimate, and what to skip. Want to see Vice City in action? Watch Yea I Really Did It on YouTube.

Estimate: about 53 million copies in week one

On November 27, Alinea Analytics' Rhys Elliott posted the firm's latest estimate, reported by Polygon and GamesRadar+. Remember, these are estimates, not official sales:

  • about 53 million copies sold in the first week, and about $4.7 billion in revenue;
  • roughly 75.7% on PS5, or about 40 million copies, against about 13 million on Xbox Series X|S;
  • about 7 million players past 50 hours, and around 250,000 past 100 hours.

GamesRadar+ notes that 53 million would already make GTA 6 the 13th best-selling game ever, passing Cyberpunk 2077. For comparison, Polygon points out that GTA 5 sold 29 million copies in its whole first month. The only official numbers are still Take-Two's: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold before launch. Take-Two has not shared a week-one figure, so treat every bigger number you see as a guess until it does. Our first-patch report has Sony's official launch-weekend stats.

Confirmed: why there is no mission replay

GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 both let you replay finished missions. GTA 6 does not, and players noticed right away. Senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku that Rockstar "certainly" considered it early in development, but "as the game came together, it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected." He added that replaying missions "really diminished the choices you had made."

What you can still do:

  • Mid-mission: open the menu to retry, restart or quit if a mission goes wrong (GameSpot).
  • After a mission: load an older save. Our tip: keep a few manual saves before big story missions, so you can go back to your favorites without redoing hours of play.

Rockstar has not said if replay will ever be added in an update.

Confirmed: how the ending works (light spoilers)

Skip this section if you want to go in completely blind. In an interview with The Verge, producer and Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson said a classic "keep playing after the credits" ending did not fit Jason and Lucia's story: "Their story needed to end and have some kind of conclusion."

So the post-story game works differently. You return to a point before a big late-game decision, with what Nelson calls "the best single-player safehouse you've ever had in a GTA": a garage full of supercars, a helipad and a gym. From there you can play as long as you want. The ending can change based on how much money you have saved, what kind of criminal you were, and choices in the final moments. Nelson says there is "a large number of potential outcomes based on how you played it." That also explains the no-replay decision: your choices are supposed to stick.

Black Friday: no official discount

If you were waiting for a sale, there is no official one. As of November 28, GamesRadar+'s live deals tracker shows GTA 6 still at $79.99 at the big US stores (Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, GameStop, Target). The only US discount it found was at a digital code seller, around $71.99. UK stores have deeper cuts. Our advice: buy only from stores you trust, and check that a code matches your console and region before you pay. The Ultimate edition is still $99.99 on the PlayStation Store.

Rockstar's UK staff vote against union recognition

VGC reports that a ballot at Rockstar's UK studios, which ran from November 12 to 26, ended with about 75% against recognizing the IWGB's Rockstar Game Workers Union and about 25% in favor. The IWGB says it will keep organizing. Rockstar's head of publishing, Jennifer Kolbe, said the result "reflects our team's strong support" for direct relationships with management.

Still true: single-player only, no official PC version

The PlayStation Store FAQ still calls GTA 6 "a single-player experience," and Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 Online mode or a date for one. There is also still no official PC version. Polygon reports that fan ports of the leaked Xbox build are progressing fast, but those are unauthorized, and downloading leaked builds is piracy and a common way to get malware. We do not link to them.
