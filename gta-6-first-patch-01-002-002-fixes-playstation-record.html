GTA 6's first patch is here: every fix in update 01.002.002, what it didn't fix, and PlayStation's record launch

Topics:
  • Updates & fixes
  • Launch & sales
  • Cheats & glitches

Rockstar's first post-launch update fixes autosaves, stuck weather and missing story calls. Here is the full list, what is still broken, Sony's record numbers and the PC news to be careful with.

Updated November 25, 2026. GTA 6 has its first post-launch patch, and for the first time Rockstar has published patch notes. The update targets the bugs players complained about most over launch weekend: autosaves that did not stick, story missions that never unlocked because a phone call never came, and Vice City weather that stayed sunny for hours. Sony also shared its own launch numbers, and the PC situation got messier. Here is what is confirmed, what is only reported, and what to ignore. Want to see the game in action? Watch Yea I Really Did It on YouTube.

Confirmed: the seven fixes in Rockstar's patch notes

Rockstar posted the notes on November 23 on its support site, on a page called "Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Updates (PS5 / XBOX Series X|S)". They did not appear on the Newswire. The list is short. The update:

  • fixes several causes of crashes;
  • fixes weather not changing for long stretches;
  • fixes the game not autosaving correctly;
  • fixes phone calls not triggering, including calls that move the story forward or unlock missions;
  • fixes being unable to lose a Wanted Level;
  • fixes the gym deadlift bar not working;
  • fixes partners not handing over bags you need to sell to fences.

That is the whole list. There are no new features or performance modes. Game Informer, GamesRadar+ and TechRadar all reported the same seven fixes.

Reported: version number and download size

Rockstar's notes do not give a version number or a size. Polygon and tracker accounts report it as 01.002.002 on PS5 (about 604 MB) and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S (about 1.6 GB), released in stages through November 23. If you do not see it, check for an update manually: on PS5, highlight the game, press Options and pick Check for Update.

Our tip: even with the autosave fix, save manually before big missions until you are sure your own save behaves. Rockstar has not said whether a story call you missed before the patch will now show up on an older save. If a mission still will not unlock, play some side content and let in-game time pass first.

Not fixed yet: the money glitch and CE-108255-1

The patch notes do not mention exploits. Notebookcheck and IBTimes UK both report that the red barn money glitch still works after the update, and IBTimes says some players are still reporting a Chapter 3 softlock involving Jason. The notes also do not name the PS5 crash error CE-108255-1, so we cannot say if the general crash fixes cover it. Our glitches guide and the fix finder on our launch-day guide have the details. Rockstar has not said when the next update is coming.

Confirmed: the biggest launch in PlayStation history

On November 23, the PlayStation Blog called GTA 6 the biggest game launch in PlayStation history. Sony's own data for November 19 to 22 says:

  • more than 39 million unique players played it on PS5;
  • it set a new concurrent-player record for any game on PlayStation;
  • players logged 683 million hours, an average of 17.4 hours each;
  • it was the first game played on 70% of new PS5s since launch.

Analyst firms have their own numbers, and they are estimates, not official sales. Alinea Analytics, via Polygon, puts launch-weekend sales at about 44.9 million copies and $4 billion. Sensor Tower, via Forbes, says about 47.8 million. The only official figure is still Take-Two's: $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold before release, which we covered in our first-weekend report.

Rockstar on difficulty: "somewhere in the middle"

Some players say GTA 6 is too easy. GameSpot reports that Rockstar's Rob Nelson told Destructoid the studio's data showed GTA 4 was "a little too punishing in places" and GTA 5 "perhaps too easy," so the team aimed to "land somewhere in the middle." There are still no difficulty settings, but Nelson says some open-world content, like compounds and fronts, is tougher on purpose.

Be careful: GTA 6 "on PC"

There is still no official PC version. Rockstar Support lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. What you are seeing online are fan attempts to run a leaked Xbox build on Windows. The Verge reports that Take-Two sent a DMCA takedown that removed the biggest of these projects on GitHub, along with more than 2,900 copies of it. Downloading leaked builds is piracy and a common way to get malware, so we do not link to them. If Rockstar announces a PC release, we will cover it here.
