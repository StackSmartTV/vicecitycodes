GTA 6 Glitches & Exploits (Updated Nov 20): Money Glitch, First-Person Mode & More

The red barn money glitch, a first-person camera trick, a Key Lento store-robbery reset, and launch-day bugs, with verified or unconfirmed labels. Story mode only; back up your save.

Last updated November 20, 2026. A day after launch, GTA 6 already has a working money loop, a first-person camera trick, a store-robbery reset, and a few physics bugs that are mostly funny. Everything here is story mode only. GTA Online is not out yet, so the ban risk is low, but you should still back up a manual save before you try the money exploits. Rockstar has shipped updates 1.002.000 and 1.002.001 with no patch notes, so we cannot tell from official notes which bugs those builds already fixed. We re-checked these reports today and will update the badges when a patch lands. Watch us try them on Yea I Really Did It.

Red barn infinite money glitch Verified

Drive your personal vehicle (you need the trunk) north of Key Lento into South Vice-Dale County, east of Hamlet, and look for the red barn at Apollo Aggregates. Loot the cocaine on the back-office table and in the safe, then grab the cash stash bag from the open van in the middle of the barn and top it up with cocaine from the nearby vans. Put both bags in your trunk, make a manual save (an autosave is not enough), and reload it. You wake up at Jason's house with the bags still in the car, and the barn's loot comes back. Game Rant and Beebom both ran the loop; two bags are worth about $8,640, and Beebom counted about $11,280 on the first clear. If the loot does not return immediately, reload a few more times or wait a few in-game days.

When your trunk is full, around four bags, take them to Brian's Lockup in southwest Key Lento, enter keypad code 7700, and drop the bags inside. Brian fences the goods after a few in-game days, and the cash shows up in the mailbox at Jason's safehouse. This was still unpatched as of November 20, but early patches often remove exploits like this, so watch the badge. Related: our GTA 6 cheats list.

First-person mode glitch Verified

GTA 6 has no official first-person option, but a camera glitch can lock you into that view. In Settings > Controller, set the Touch Pad button (PS5) or View button (Xbox) to Camera instead of Map. Sit in a vehicle, open the in-game phone with D-pad Up, then close it with Circle or B while mashing the touchpad or View button. It can take several tries; Beebom needed more than ten. Once it sticks, you can walk, drive, and use weapons, though some animations look unfinished. There is no clean toggle back: reload your save to return to third-person, so save first. Dexerto and Beebom both walked through the steps, and it was still unpatched on November 20.

Key Lento store-robbery reset Verified

Robbed shops normally close for a cooldown. A way around that has now been shown in videos and in a write-up from Polygon: rob the stores around Key Lento, lose the cops, then start a story mission that begins with a cutscene, such as Meet Brian. During the cutscene, pause and choose Restart Mission. When it starts again, pause and choose Quit Mission. The stores reopen, and you keep the cash. The steps first spread in YouTube videos listed by VicePulse. Sleeping or changing clothes does not reset the shops. This one looks likely to be patched too.

Fun launch bugs (not exploits)

Most launch-day bugs are physics and clipping moments, not money makers. Beebom's bug tracker and Sportskeeda have been collecting the clips:

  • The swingset launch is back. Verified Hitting a tree at speed can fling a car across the map, like GTA 4's swingset glitch. The car usually wrecks.
  • Falling under the map. Verified In the Prologue, Lucia has been seen clipping through the ground after getting tased. A supercar crash has also left a player stuck under the map.
  • A phone call into the void. Verified If you pull out the phone while Jason is wedged between a car and a fence, he can drop through the world. Beebom says you may have to restart the game, so save first.
  • Jumping from a plane with no parachute. Verified One clip shows Jason taking no fall damage after leaping out without a chute. Treat it as a bug, not a reliable method.
  • Escaping the first arrest. Unconfirmed Streamer Kai Cenat appeared to escape the scripted first-mission arrest after a bugged animation. Gulf News reported the clip, but it is not clear whether that was a bug or an intended outcome.

Scam warning: fake "infinite money glitch" posts

Viral posts that promise an "infinite money glitch" if you open a car browser, visit a casino site, or buy a coin called $GLITCH are not real game exploits. They are gambling ads, memecoin promotions, or spam. A website cannot add cash to a PS5 or Xbox save, and GTA 6 has no online money drops yet. The only official cash method is the $250,000 cheat, which unlocks after you beat the story and turns off saving for that session. If a clip asks for a PlayStation, Xbox, or Rockstar login, close it.
