GTA 6's first weekend: a record $3.79 billion launch, 8-hour play sessions, the Miami festival and a PS5 crash to watch

Topics:
  • Launch & sales
  • Updates & fixes
  • Events & music

Take-Two's official day-one numbers, Circana's launch-day playtime data, what happened at The Festival in Miami, the CE-108255-1 PS5 crash, and the rumors we could not confirm.

Updated November 22, 2026. GTA 6 has been out for three days, and the first official numbers are in. Take-Two says it is the biggest entertainment launch ever, research firm Circana says the average player put in a full workday on launch day, and Rockstar threw a festival in Miami last night. Not everything is good news, though: a PS5 crash is stopping some players from finishing early missions, and a wave of patch and PC rumors is spreading faster than the facts. Here is what is confirmed, what is only reported, and what is just noise. Watch our first-weekend reactions on Yea I Really Did It.

Confirmed: $3.79 billion on day one

On November 20, Take-Two Interactive announced that GTA 6's day-one retail sales reached $3.79 billion, which it called a record for entertainment "across any medium." The same release says the game sold 32.5 million units before release, a record across all platforms, and repeats the 99 Metascore we covered at launch. Take-Two notes that the number is a company estimate through the end of November 19, worldwide, so it is not an audited figure yet. The AP and Polygon both reported the announcement the same day. Polygon also points out that earlier third-party estimates were lower, around 25 million copies, so treat outside tracker numbers as rough guesses.

Reported: players averaged more than 8 hours on launch day

Circana's Mat Piscatella shared launch-day data from the company's Player Engagement Tracker. Among players who were active on November 19, 61% of US PS5 players and 56% of Xbox players worldwide played GTA 6. Those players averaged 513 minutes on PS5 (about 8.5 hours) and 494 minutes on Xbox. Two cautions: these are percentages of active users that day, not of every console owner, and the PS5 and Xbox numbers cover different regions, so they are not a head-to-head comparison. Piscatella called the numbers "absolutely bonkers."

Confirmed: The Album is out and The Festival happened

Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album on November 20. It is on the major streaming services, with vinyl and CD editions. On Saturday, November 21, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival took over Mana Wynwood in Miami with Rockstar and PlayStation. Miami New Times reports sets from Cardi B, Justice, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Nick León b2b DJ Python, Rauw Alejandro, Metro Boomin and Travis Scott with his Cactus Jack crew, plus real-life versions of in-game spots like Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell. Rockstar has not announced a replay or a second date. The Justice score album is still set for December 11, and The Game Awards, where GTA 6 has 8 nominations, are on December 10 (see our launch calendar).

Watch out: PS5 crash error CE-108255-1

GameSpot, Polygon and Eurogamer have all written up reports of GTA 6 crashing to the PS5 home screen with error CE-108255-1. Some players hit it in the prologue, others after about 40 minutes, and one widely shared report says it happens every time during the early mission Pipework. GameSpot says the problem does not look very widespread, and as of this morning neither Rockstar nor Sony has acknowledged it or announced a patch. Sony's page for the code just says an error occurred with the game and suggests updating it.

What players are trying: check for a game update (Options > Check for Update), update the PS5 system software, Restore Licenses, use Safe Mode to clear the cache and rebuild the database, or reinstall the game on internal storage. Polygon reports that some players got past the crash by changing the PS5 video output resolution from Manual to 1080p. None of these is an official fix, so save manually and often until Rockstar responds. Our launch-day guide has the fix finder for other launch problems.

Not confirmed: a "Nov 25 patch" and GTA 6 on PC

Update, Nov 25: Rockstar released its first post-launch update on November 23 (not November 25), with official patch notes. See every fix in update 01.002.002.

A viral post claims Rockstar apologized for lag and promised a big update on November 25. We checked the Rockstar Newswire and Rockstar Support this morning and found no such announcement. The only updates so far are the two pre-launch ones, 01.002.000 and 01.002.001, and Rockstar has published no patch notes for either. You may also see claims that GTA 6 is "coming to PC next week." That comes from an anonymous leaker who posted files from an older Xbox build, not from Rockstar. XDA reports the files are missing the game's executable, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version. Do not download leaked builds: they can carry malware, and the leak is tied to a crypto token promotion. Need cheats while you wait for a patch?
