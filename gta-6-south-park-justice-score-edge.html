GTA 6 on South Park, a Justice score album and Edge's big reveals: the week in GTA 6

Real GTA 6 gameplay aired on South Park, Rockstar dated the Justice score for Dec 11, and Edge detailed the Criminal Profile and police heat. Here is what is confirmed.

  2. Justice's original score gets an album on December 11

Less than three weeks before Grand Theft Auto VI arrives on November 19, 2026, Rockstar had one of its busiest weeks yet. New gameplay turned up in a very unlikely place, the game's score got its own album, and Edge's cover story explained how GTA 6 will judge the kind of criminal you are. Here is a sourced rundown, with anything still unconfirmed clearly marked.

Real GTA 6 gameplay aired on South Park

The October 28 episode of South Park, "Guinea Pig on a Stick," included just under 20 seconds of genuine GTA 6 gameplay. In the clip, Jason and Lucia walk along a crowded Vice City beach. A dog walker bags his dog's mess, Jason throws something at him, and when the man turns to fight, Jason grabs the bag and hits him with it. Yes, really.

It is not a South Park parody. IGN reports that the end credits read "Grand Theft Auto VI footage courtesy of Rockstar Games," and Eurogamer says it verified the footage. Beyond the joke, the clip is another look at how packed Rockstar's beaches are with NPCs. Rockstar itself has not commented on the clip, and it is not on Rockstar's own channels.

Justice's original score gets an album on December 11

On October 29, Rockstar's Newswire announced Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score by Justice, the Grammy-winning French duo of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay. Rockstar calls it an adaptive score, meaning the music is tuned to react to how you play across Vice City and Leonida. The album comes out December 11, 2026 on streaming, CD and vinyl, about three weeks after the game.

The same day, Justice released a new single, "Pusher" featuring Gesaffelstein, a roughly seven-minute track. The score also includes "Flower in a Hurricane," the song from the October 15 Launch Trailer. Don't mix this up with Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the separate 34-track collection of songs by different artists that comes out on November 19 alongside the game. Consequence reports the score has 30 tracks plus bonus cuts. Rockstar has not said where "Pusher" plays in the game.

Edge: your Criminal Profile shapes the story

Edge's issue 430 cover feature, published online by GamesRadar+ on October 29, is based on a 90-minute session where Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson played the current build. The biggest reveal is the Criminal Profile. It tracks Jason and Lucia on a scale from "Professional" to "Irredeemable," shown with angel and devil icons. You can knock enemies out or intimidate them instead of killing them, and Rockstar says your profile will influence how the story unfolds. GamesHub adds that Jason and Lucia's relationship sits on a four-point scale that grows through time spent together. Rockstar would not say whether they can marry.

Cops now remember what they saw

The police are smarter too. A new "heat" system shows icons for what the cops know: your description, your clothes, your weapon, whether you had a partner and whether CCTV caught you. Change your outfit, ditch the weapon or split up and you get harder to recognize. Polygon reports that police use K9 units and spike strips, and in Edge's demo a police dog pinned Jason after a crash. Nelson told Edge the game is "ready," and Rockstar provided 12 new screenshots for the feature.

What has not changed

  • Launch: November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC version has not been announced.
  • Pre-load: opens November 12 for digital pre-orders, according to Rockstar Support.
  • Price: $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate (Take-Two).
  • File size: still unofficial. The reported PS5 Standard size is 210 GB. Sony, Microsoft and Rockstar have not listed one yet. Check if it fits on your console.
  • Not confirmed: claims that review copies show each reviewer's name on screen, and a reported November 18 review date. Treat both as rumors for now.
