GTA 5 Cheats FAQ

Do GTA 5 cheats disable trophies and achievements?

Yes, but only for the current play session. While a cheat is active you can't earn trophies or achievements. Restart the game or reload a save and they work again. You can still save while cheats are on, but keep a clean save just in case.

Do GTA 5 cheats work in GTA Online?

No. Cheat codes only work in GTA 5 Story Mode. They can't be used in GTA Online.

How do I enter GTA 5 cheats on PC?

Press the tilde key (~), the key left of 1, to open the cheat box, type the code (for example TURTLE) and press Enter. You can also dial the phone numbers on the in-game phone or use the Xbox button combos with a controller.

How do GTA 5 phone cheats work?

Open the in-game phone, go to Contacts, open the dial pad and dial the number, such as 1-999-887-853 for max health and armor. Phone cheats work on PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S and PC, not on PS3 or Xbox 360.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 5?

No. GTA 5 has no cheat for money, so be careful with any site or video that claims one exists.

Why does the game say Cheat Denied?

Many cheats can't be used during missions or certain activities. Leave the mission and try again in free roam.

How do I turn a cheat off?

Some cheats switch off when you enter them again. Others wear off, end when your character dies, or clear when you reload a save.

Are there GTA 6 cheat codes yet?

No official GTA 6 cheat codes have been confirmed. We'll add them once they're verified after launch.
