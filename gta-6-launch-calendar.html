GTA 6 launch calendar: every date that matters, from Nov 9 to Dec 11

Take-Two earnings, pre-load, the Miami HEAT Vice City night, launch day, the pre-order bonus cutoff and the Justice album. Every confirmed GTA 6 date, plus the one that is only reported.

We are in the final stretch. Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026, and the weeks around it are packed: an earnings call, pre-load day, reviews, an NBA game played in "Vice City," launch day itself and a soundtrack album in December. Here is every date in one place, what each one means for you, and which ones are confirmed versus only reported. Save the ones you care about straight to your phone with the calendar buttons below.

Nov 9: Take-Two's earnings call

Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, reports its quarterly results after the market closes on Monday, November 9, with a conference call at 4:30 PM Eastern. It is the last scheduled call before launch, so it is the most likely place for an official update on pre-orders or launch plans. Do not expect gameplay reveals. Earnings calls are about money, not trailers, but any change to the November 19 date would almost certainly surface here. Take-Two lists a public, listen-only webcast on its investor site.

Nov 10: pre-load day (moved up from Nov 12)

Update, Nov 4: Rockstar moved digital pre-loading up two days. If you pre-ordered a digital copy, pre-loading opens on Tuesday, November 10: at local midnight on PS5, and at 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox Series X|S (7 PM Eastern on Monday, November 9). Boxed copies still go on sale on November 12. The box holds a download code rather than a disc, so you can redeem it and start downloading straight away. Rockstar now says the pre-load file needs up to 212 GB depending on your console (Rockstar Newswire). Clear space a few days early. Our pre-load guide and fit checker tell you if you are ready.

Nov 18: reviews (reported) and A Night in Vice City

Two things land the day before launch. First, the review embargo. Insider NateTheHate posted on October 26 that review copies were going out with a November 18 embargo, and Insider Gaming says it independently verified that date. Rockstar has not confirmed it, so treat it as reported. If it holds, it matches Rockstar's habit: GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 reviews both went live a day before release.

Second, and fully confirmed, is "A Night in Vice City." The Miami HEAT host the Milwaukee Bucks at Kaseya Center at 7:00 PM Eastern, playing as Vice City on a one-night-only court built for the event and wearing a completely reimagined uniform. GameSpot reports the game airs on ESPN. Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands are already open inside the arena. It is also Giannis Antetokounmpo's first game against the Bucks since his trade to Miami, according to USA Today, so expect a loud crowd. It is also the subject of our GTA 6 Hub Short going up today, "The Miami Heat Become the VICE CITY Heat?!"

Nov 19: launch day, and the bonus deadline

GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version announced and it will not run on PS4. The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. November 19 is also effectively the last day to get the Vintage Vice City Pack, because Rockstar says the bonus covers pre-orders and purchases made before November 20. Digital pre-orders also include a free month of GTA+.

Our countdown at the top of every page targets midnight Eastern. Rockstar has not published an exact unlock time for each region, so if the game unlocks earlier or later where you live, go by your console's store page.

Dec 11: the Justice score album

Three weeks after launch, Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score by Justice arrives on streaming, CD and vinyl, per the Rockstar Newswire. It is separate from Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the multi-artist collection out on launch day. You can already hear the single "Pusher" featuring Gesaffelstein.

What about cheat codes?

Rockstar has not released any GTA 6 cheats, and we will not post guesses. When real codes appear after launch, they will go up here first, verified. Until then, our GTA 5 cheat list has all 35 codes with copy buttons.
