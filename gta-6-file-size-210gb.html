GTA 6 is reportedly 210 GB: how to get ready for pre-load day

A storage tracker puts the PS5 download at 210 GB, a record share of gamers plan to buy it, and Edge has a big new feature. What is confirmed and what to do before Nov 12.

In this article (6 sections · 3 min)
  1. The 210 GB number (reported, not official)
  2. Pre-load and launch dates (confirmed)
  3. A record level of demand
  4. Edge's 16-page cover story lands October 29
  5. No, it has not been delayed again
  6. Cheat codes
  7. Sources

Three weeks out from launch, the Grand Theft Auto VI conversation has shifted from "when?" to "will it fit?". This week a storage tracker put a number on the download, a market research firm said no game has ever had more people planning to buy it, and Edge magazine announced a 16-page cover story. Here is what we know, what is still unconfirmed, and a quick checker so you can see whether your console is ready.

The 210 GB number (reported, not official)

On October 27, the PlayStation Game Size tracker posted that the PS5 Standard Edition of GTA 6 will need 210 GB of storage. GameSpot picked it up the same day and notes that sizes for the Ultimate Edition and the Xbox Series X|S version were not shared. GameSpot also points out that this is about double the size of Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has not confirmed the figure, so treat it as a strong estimate. We will update this page if an official size appears on the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or Rockstar Support.

For context, a launch PS5 has an 825 GB SSD with roughly 667 GB usable, so 210 GB would be close to a third of that space. The Xbox Series S has about 364 GB usable, so if the Xbox version is a similar size, Series S owners will feel it most.

Will GTA 6 fit on your console?

GTA 6 is reported to need 210 GB on PS5 (Standard Edition). That number comes from the PlayStation Game Size tracker, via GameSpot (Oct 27, 2026); Rockstar has not confirmed it yet.

Where do I find my free space?
  • PS5: Settings → Storage → Console Storage.
  • Xbox Series X|S: Settings → System → Storage devices.

Pre-load and launch dates (confirmed)

  • Pre-load: opens at local midnight on November 12, 2026 for digital pre-orders of either edition, according to Rockstar Support.
  • Physical copies: the box holds a download code, not a disc. Retailers start shipping on November 12, and you can redeem the code right away to pre-load.
  • Launch: Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Our countdown targets midnight Eastern.
  • Pre-order bonus: purchases before November 20 include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital pre-orders also get one month of GTA+.

Our tip: free up space a few days before November 12 instead of on the night. Downloads and day-one patches go faster when you are not deleting games at the same time.

A record level of demand

Market research firm Circana says 29% of US console and PC gamers plan to buy GTA 6, the highest purchase intent it has ever measured for any game, GameSpot reported on October 28. Circana's Mat Piscatella called the launch a "rare cultural and commercial moment" and expects it to lift sales of consoles and accessories too. GameSpot also cites Sensor Tower estimates of about 7 million copies already pre-sold. Those are third-party estimates, not Take-Two figures.

Edge's 16-page cover story lands October 29

Edge issue 430 goes on sale October 29 with a 16-page GTA 6 cover feature built on a visit to Rockstar North, including interviews and new screenshots. GamesRadar+ says the full feature will be free to read online the same day. We will pull out anything new and confirmed once it is published.

Update, Oct 31: the feature is out. Read our recap of Edge's reveals, including the new Criminal Profile and police heat system.

No, it has not been delayed again

Posts claiming GTA 6 slipped to 2027 have been circulating. As IBTimes UK noted on October 28, Rockstar and Take-Two have announced no new delay, and Rockstar still lists November 19, 2026. A PC version has also not been announced. Some outlets report one is expected in early 2027, but that comes from unnamed sources, not Rockstar.
