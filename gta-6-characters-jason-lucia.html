GTA 6 Characters: Jason, Lucia and the Confirmed Cast

Who are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos? Rockstar's official bios for GTA 6's leads and supporting cast, from Cal Hampton to Boobie Ike and Raul Bautista.

How to read the labels: Confirmed = stated by Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft. Reported = reported by a reputable outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar. Not confirmed = not announced or unknown.

Grand Theft Auto VI has two playable leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and a supporting cast that Rockstar has introduced on its official "Only in Leonida" pages Confirmed. This guide sums up what Rockstar has actually said about each character, in our own words plus a few short quotes. We have left out the fan theories. Story details beyond Rockstar's official bios are unknown until launch, and we will avoid spoilers when we update this page.

The premise

Rockstar's official summary: "Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive." Polygon sums it up as a Bonnie and Clyde-style story, and both trailers lean on that idea: a couple whose loyalty to each other is the whole point.

Jason Duval

Rockstar's tagline for Jason is "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." His bio says he grew up "around grifters and crooks," went into the Army to shake off a troubled adolescence, and ended up in the Leonida Keys "doing what he knows best, working for local drug runners." Rockstar adds that meeting Lucia "could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him" Confirmed.

What else is official: Jason is living rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties in exchange for help with "local shakedowns" Confirmed. The Ultimate Edition gives him personalized gear, including an engraved Girardi ES9 pistol and a retro build for his "well-worn Vapid Ganado low-riding pickup," plus "Jason's safehouse vehicles," an Army-fatigue Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak Confirmed. Those small details paint a picture of a working-class guy on the edge of the Keys' smuggling scene, not a kingpin.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia's tagline is "Lucia's father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk." Rockstar says that fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary and that "sheer luck" got her out. She has "learned her lesson — only smart moves from here." More than anything, she wants the good life her mother has dreamed of "since their days in Liberty City." That is a nice nod to the series' New York stand-in. Rockstar's bio ends with "A life with Jason could be her way out" Confirmed.

Lucia is widely described as the series' first female lead of the modern era. Very early top-down GTA games let you pick from a roster of characters that included women, so "first ever" is not quite right. Her Ultimate Edition item is a personalized Klose K17 pistol, and the Vintage Vice City Pack gives her a red sequin mini dress and curls Confirmed.

How the two leads work together

Rockstar has not explained exactly how switching between Jason and Lucia works in the finished game Not confirmed. GTA 5 let you swap between three protagonists almost anywhere, but that does not mean GTA 6 copies it. Edge's preview describes a Criminal Profile that tracks both characters from "Professional" to "Irredeemable," and outlets covering the feature say their relationship grows over time spent together Reported. Rockstar would not say whether the two can marry, according to that coverage.

The supporting cast Rockstar has introduced

  • Cal Hampton. Jason's friend and "a fellow associate of Brian's." He feels safest at home, "snooping on Coast Guard comms with a few beers and some private browser tabs open." His tagline, "What if everything on the internet was true?", tells you he is the conspiracy-theory friend.
  • Boobie Ike. "A local Vice City legend — and acts like it." Rockstar says he turned his time on the streets into a legitimate empire of real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. He is most invested in his partnership with Dre'Quan in Only Raw Records.
  • Dre'Quan Priest. "Always more of a hustler than a gangster." An aspiring music mogul whose goal was always to break into music. He has signed the Real Dimez and has his sights set on the wider Vice City scene.
  • Real Dimez. Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since high school, who turned "shaking down local dealers" into rap tracks and a big social media presence. An early hit with local rapper DWNPLY took them to new heights, and now they are signed to Only Raw Records.
  • Raul Bautista. "A seasoned bank robber always on the hunt for talent." Rockstar warns that his "recklessness raises the stakes with every score."
  • Brian Heder. "A classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys," still moving product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori. He is the one letting Jason live rent-free.

All of the above comes from Rockstar's official character pages Confirmed. Any role these characters play in specific missions is unknown until launch.

Other names in official material

A few more characters appear in Rockstar's edition text. Wyman is an "eccentric collector and local fixer" who hands out the Ultimate Edition's Classic Car Collection commission. Macca the Gator is a cartoon star from Leonida's in-universe TV, with his own show, Macca & Friends, on the Weazel network. And there is a nod to Tommy Vercetti, the hero of 2002's GTA: Vice City: the pre-order weapon pattern is inspired by his palm-tree shirt, and the Ultimate Edition revolvers are "sourced from the Vercetti Estate" Confirmed. Whether Tommy himself appears is not confirmed Not confirmed.

Voice cast

Rockstar has not officially announced the voice and performance-capture cast on its GTA VI pages Not confirmed. You will see names online. We will add the official credits once they are public, which with Rockstar usually means at launch.
