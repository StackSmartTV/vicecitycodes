GTA 6 Launch Gallery drops, Zelnick talks delays, and Take-Two's last call before launch

Rockstar opened The Launch Gallery with a fresh batch of screenshots, Take-Two's CEO addressed delays and piracy at WSJ Tech, and Monday's earnings call is the last scheduled investor update before November 19.

Update, Nov 10: Take-Two's Monday call is done (pre-orders "unprecedented," full-year outlook kept), pre-load is now live, and Rockstar announced a Nov 21 launch festival in Miami. Read our Nov 10 recap.

Two weeks out from Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19, 2026, Rockstar just gave the map another long look. On November 5 the studio opened The Launch Gallery on the official GTA VI site: a collection of new marketing screenshots of Vice City, Jason, Lucia and the wider Leonida map. Separately, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick spoke at WSJ Tech about the game's delays and about piracy, and on Monday, November 9 at 4:30 PM Eastern Take-Two holds its last scheduled earnings call before launch. Here is what is confirmed, what is imagery only, and what still has not changed.

What The Launch Gallery is (and isn't)

Rockstar's Newswire post on November 5 was titled Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery. The studio's own blurb frames it simply: GTA VI is "officially only two weeks away," and the gallery is "a stunning look at Vice City and beyond with a collection of new screenshots on the official site." You can browse and download them from rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery and the wider media hub.

Outlet counts of the new batch land around the high thirties (Endgame Tools listed about 38; other roundups said about 39). Rockstar itself has not published an official count in the Newswire blurb we can cite, so treat those as outlet tallies, not a Rockstar number. Call it about three dozen new shots.

What shows up is familiar ground with new angles: Vice City by day and night, Jason and Lucia, Grassrivers, the Leonida Keys and Port Gellhorn. Alt text and file names call out scenes such as a Fleeca bank robbery and a night at The Rusty Anchor. That is marketing imagery. It is not a systems deep-dive, not a new feature list, and not a change to the November 19 date.

Zelnick at WSJ Tech: delays were expensive, and right

Speaking at WSJ Tech (November 4; covered November 5–6 by GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer), Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick defended the GTA 6 delays as "expensive and inconvenient" but necessary in service of making the best entertainment. He pointed to a high-profile release that shipped unfinished and then had to be pulled. He did not name the title. Outlets have different guesses: GamesRadar+ read it as a Cyberpunk 2077 situation; PC Gamer bets on Concord. Those are outlets' readings, not Zelnick's words.

Piracy as "a fact of life"

On piracy, Zelnick called it "a fact of life." He compared it to other media: the movie business has lived with roughly 10–20% piracy, and music in the mid-2000s saw rates around 80%. Large file sizes and game complexity reduce the problem but do not eliminate it. If piracy becomes a real problem for GTA 6, he said Take-Two turns first to legal measures, then technical ones. That is a measured stance two weeks before launch, not a claim that the game cannot be pirated.

Monday: Take-Two's last call before launch

Take-Two reports its second-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Monday, November 9, 2026, with a conference call at 4:30 PM Eastern. The quarter ended September 30, so these numbers will not include any post-launch GTA 6 sales. It is still the last scheduled investor call before November 19, which makes it the most likely place for an official word on pre-orders or launch readiness. Do not expect a gameplay reveal; earnings calls are about money. Take-Two lists a public webcast on its investor site.

Reminder: pre-load is Tuesday

Digital pre-loading still opens on Tuesday, November 10: midnight local time on PS5, and 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox Series X|S (7 PM Eastern on Monday, November 9). Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB. Boxed code-in-box copies arrive November 12. Full times and the storage checker are in our pre-load guide.
