GTA 6 Release Date, Platforms, Price and Pre-Load: The Confirmed Facts

GTA 6 release date and unlock times, PS5 and Xbox versions, PC status, $79.99 and $99.99 editions, code-in-box copies and the 212 GB pre-load, all sourced.

How to read the labels: Confirmed = stated by Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft. Reported = reported by a reputable outlet, not confirmed by Rockstar. Not confirmed = not announced or unknown.

Update, November 19, 2026: GTA 6 is out now on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See our launch-day guide for fixes, reviews and the free map.

This page answers the practical questions about getting Grand Theft Auto VI on day one: when it comes out, which consoles it runs on, what it costs, how pre-loading works and what is in the box. Everything here comes from Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft unless it is labelled otherwise, and we have kept rumors out. Sources are at the bottom.

Release date: Thursday, November 19, 2026

GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026 Confirmed. That date has held since November 6, 2025, when Rockstar delayed the game for the second time. The game was first planned for 2025, then moved to May 26, 2026, and then to November. Rockstar's statement at the time said the extra months "will allow us to finish the game with the level of polish you have come to expect and deserve." Take-Two executives said on the November 9, 2026 earnings call that the game is finished, as reported by GamesBeat, and the official site still reads "Coming November 19, 2026."

On PlayStation 5, Sony says launch is a rolling midnight by country. It begins at 12:00 AM NZDT in New Zealand, so players there get in first, and moves around the globe as each country hits midnight on November 19 Confirmed. Rockstar has not published a detailed unlock table for every US time zone, so we are not going to guess at one. On Xbox, Xbox Cloud Gaming streams start at 1 AM Eastern on November 19 Confirmed.

Platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

  • PlayStation 5 and PS5 Pro: yes Confirmed. PS5 Pro adds PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution for a sharper image and ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets Confirmed.
  • Xbox Series X and Series S: yes, native versions you can pre-install Confirmed.
  • Xbox One: only through Xbox Cloud Gaming, which uses Series S graphics settings. You still need to buy the game Confirmed.
  • PlayStation 4: no. PlayStation's FAQ says GTA 6 will only be playable on PS5 Confirmed.
  • PC: not announced Not confirmed. Rockstar Support lists only PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Some outlets cite unnamed sources about a later PC release, but neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has announced one, so treat any PC date you see as a rumor.
  • Nintendo Switch 2: not announced Not confirmed.

Rockstar's stated performance target is 30 frames per second on all consoles, chosen to keep Leonida's density and detail intact, according to the technical notes Rockstar shared at pre-load time Confirmed. There is no confirmed 60 fps mode, including on PS5 Pro.

Price and editions

In the US, the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99, according to Take-Two's June 24, 2026 press release Confirmed. Prices in other countries vary by store and currency, so check your local PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store listing.

  • Standard Edition: the full game.
  • Ultimate Edition: the full game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, an in-game bundle of vehicles, weapons, outfits, shops and activities that Rockstar says unlocks across the story's chapters Confirmed.
  • Upgrade later: Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade separately at any point, including boxed-copy owners once their code is redeemed Confirmed.
  • Pre-order bonus: all pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack. Digital pre-orders also get one free month of GTA+ Confirmed.

Digital vs. boxed copies

This is the part that catches people out: the boxed version has no disc. Rockstar Support says the physical version contains a download code for the digital game, and it became available from retailers starting November 12 Confirmed. There is no physical Ultimate Edition. If you buy a code-in-box copy, redeem it as soon as you get it to start downloading. Rockstar Support also explains that PlayStation codes only work on accounts from the matching country group (for example, North American codes on US and Canadian accounts), while Xbox codes are not region-locked Confirmed.

Several outlets also noted that boxed copies do not include a printed map, which disappointed some long-time fans. Rockstar's November 3 Newswire post said a printable cover poster and a map of Leonida will be added to the official site as a free download on November 19, with a double-sided printed version sold at the Rockstar Store afterward.

Pre-load: dates and file size

Digital pre-loading opened on November 10, 2026 Confirmed. On PS5 it opened at midnight local time. On Xbox Series X|S it opened worldwide at 12 a.m. GMT, which was 7 PM Eastern on Monday, November 9. Rockstar first said pre-load would start on November 12, then moved it two days earlier. Sony said GTA 6 had become the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history and urged everyone to pre-load as soon as possible. Microsoft's Xbox Wire called it the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store Confirmed.

The download needs up to 212 GB depending on your console Confirmed. A pre-launch update, version 1.002.000, then started rolling out on November 13 at about 1 GB Reported. Rockstar has not published patch notes. Leave automatic updates on and keep your console in Rest Mode (PS5) or Sleep Mode (Xbox) so the game is fully ready before midnight.

Rating and content

The official site lists the following content descriptors: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol, and it notes In-Game Purchases Confirmed. In other words, this is a game for adults, like every mainline GTA before it. Parents can use the console's family settings to restrict mature games.

What we will add after launch

Once GTA 6 is out we will add the final installed size on each console, any day-one patch notes Rockstar publishes, and any official word on a PC version. If any of the facts above change, we will update this page and its "last updated" date. For the bigger picture, read our beginner's guide or check every date in the launch calendar.
