GTA 6 Cheat Codes (Updated Launch Day): Every Verified Code So Far

Status on November 18, 2026: GTA 6 has 61 cheats, and 17 codes are verified Verified. Each input below was published identically by at least two of PC Gamer, GameSpot and PowerPyx, which all played the game ahead of launch. The other 44 inputs have not been published yet. Rockstar has not posted an official cheat list. We will keep updating this page as players find more.

The catch: beat the story first

This is the biggest change from GTA 5. Cheats do nothing until you have finished all 91 story missions. PC Gamer and GameSpot both tested the codes on an earlier save, and they did not work. After the credits, a Cheats list appears under Progress menu > Cheats. On top of that, most cheats need their own challenge, like winning shooting-range medals or finding collectibles. The game counts challenges you already completed during the story.

Two more rules: using a cheat disables saving for that session, and it also disables trophies and achievements. Save first, then play around, then quit without saving.

How to enter GTA 6 cheats

  1. Press Up on the D-pad to bring up your phone.
  2. Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) and press R1 or RB.
  3. Enter the D-pad input from the tables below.

Inputs are the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. GTA 6 is not on PC, so there are no PC cheats.

Beware of fake cheat lists

Many "GTA 6 cheats" pages still recycle GTA 5 codes like PAINKILLER and TURTLE. Those do nothing in GTA 6. Real cheats never need your PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar password, a download, or a "money generator". Cheats are single-player only and do not work in any online mode. Looking for the old codes?
