How GTA cheats have worked, game by game

The 3D era (GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas). On consoles you entered a quick sequence of button presses during gameplay. On PC you typed a word while playing. In 2002's Vice City, for example, typing ASPIRINE refilled your health and THUGSTOOLS gave you a weapon set. Cheats were a big part of the fun, and many affected saving or unlocked silly effects like flying cars.

GTA IV (2008). Rockstar moved cheats into the in-game phone. You dialed a number to spawn a vehicle or refill health and armor. It was more immersive and the same on every platform.

GTA V (2013 onward). GTA V supported both systems. Console button combos work during gameplay, and the in-game phone accepts "1-999" numbers. On PC you can also open a cheat box with the tilde (~) key and type a word. Cheats only work in Story Mode, never in GTA Online, and while a cheat is active you cannot earn trophies or achievements for that play session.

Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). Rockstar's most recent open world did something different. You unlocked cheats by finding them in in-game newspapers and other sources, then typed short phrases into a cheats menu in the settings. Some only worked after you had progressed in the story.

Cheats, saves and GTA Online: the rules that applied in GTA 5

If GTA 6 follows GTA 5, three rules are worth knowing before you type anything. First, cheats are a single-player feature. GTA Online never accepted them, and trying to get an unfair edge online with third-party tools can get an account banned. Second, in GTA 5 a cheat turns off trophies and achievements for the rest of that play session, and reloading the game turns them back on. Third, some cheats cannot be used during certain missions, and a few, like the Dodo seaplane or Kraken sub spawns, only work after you have unlocked them in the story. We will confirm which of these rules carry over to GTA 6 once we can test it Not confirmed.

A good habit from every Rockstar game: before you experiment, save to a separate slot. That way a wanted level you cannot shake or a helicopter stuck on a roof never ruins your main playthrough.

Beware of fake cheat sites and "free money" offers

Big launches attract scams. Be wary of anything that asks you to log in with your PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar account to "unlock" cheats, promises free in-game money, or asks you to download a file. Real cheats never need your password. If a site asks for it, close the tab. And stay away from anything that claims to offer the game itself early or for free. That is piracy, and it is a common way to spread malware.
