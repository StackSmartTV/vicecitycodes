GTA 6 takes over Miami: Burger Shot, the first TV ad, and a "Night in Vice City"

Rockstar is turning real-life Miami into Vice City ahead of the Nov 19 launch. Here is what is confirmed, what fans spotted, and what has not changed.

With less than four weeks to go before Grand Theft Auto VI launches on November 19, 2026, Rockstar's marketing has left the screen and moved into the real city that inspired Vice City. Over the past week, the game's fictional brands started showing up across Miami, from the Heat's arena to Miami Beach, and the first GTA 6 TV commercial aired during NBA opening night. Here is a quick, sourced rundown of what actually happened, and what it means if you are counting down to launch.

Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are real now (sort of)

The series' long-running fast-food parodies, Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, opened as food stands inside the Kaseya Center, the home of the Miami Heat. According to GameSpot, the Burger Shot counter is the arena's existing Skinny Louie's burger stand in GTA branding, with staff in Burger Shot shirts and hats, so the food is not a recipe from the game. It is not cheap either: GameSpot lists the cheapest combo (cheeseburger, fries and a drink) at $28.50 before tax, with an applewood bacon combo at $31. Fans also spotted elevator ads inside the arena for in-game names like Vice City FM, the Vault nightclub and the TV show Macca & Friends.

These stands were reported from fan photos at Heat games, and for now they appear to be limited to the Kaseya Center.

A "Night in Vice City" on launch eve

The arena tie-in builds toward a bigger moment. GameSpot and VGC report that on November 18, the night before launch, the Heat will play a themed game dubbed "A Night in Vice City," wearing reimagined uniforms on a specially built "Vice City Court." Before the food stands opened, Rockstar had already airlifted a Vice City logo onto the top of the arena.

Vice City branding all over town

VGC rounded up what fans have photographed around the city: Vice City-branded pink and blue umbrellas and loungers on Miami Beach, a GTA 6 blimp, branding on buses and taxis, boat billboards for the fictional Vault Nightclub, and custom cars with Vice City plates, including a wrapped Coquette and a Lamborghini in Vice Beach Police livery. Ads for in-game brands such as Weazel News, Chamber 106.6, Fruit, eCola and Pißwasser have also appeared on screens and posters. Most of these sightings come from social media, so treat any single photo with care, but the overall push has been covered by several outlets.

The first GTA 6 TV ad

On October 20, the first GTA 6 commercial ran during NBA opening-night broadcasts. Kotaku reports it is essentially a shorter cut of the Launch Trailer Rockstar released on October 15, with a few of the steamier shower shots trimmed for TV. Reports say the spot's end card shows only the PlayStation logo. That is a marketing deal, not a platform change: GTA 6 is still coming to both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

If you missed it, the Launch Trailer itself debuted "Flower in a Hurricane," a new song from the game's original score composers Justice featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It followed Rockstar's 26-minute "Extended Look" gameplay video from August 27 and the reveal of the first six in-game radio stations on October 8.

What has not changed

  • Release date: Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (our countdown above targets midnight Eastern).
  • Price: Standard Edition $79.99, Ultimate Edition $99.99.
  • Pre-load: digital pre-orders can start pre-loading on November 12, 2026. Physical copies, which contain a download code, also go on sale that day.
  • Pre-order bonus: pre-orders and purchases before November 20 include the Vintage Vice City Pack.
  • Cheat codes: Rockstar has not published any GTA 6 cheats yet. We will post verified codes here as soon as they exist, and never guesses.

Why it matters

Rockstar rarely does real-world stunts at this scale. Turning Miami itself into a billboard, a TV spot four weeks out and a themed NBA game on launch eve all point to one thing: the November 19 date looks locked in. If you are planning your launch week, now is a good time to clear console storage before pre-loading opens on November 12.
